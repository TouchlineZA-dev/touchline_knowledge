# Working with Touchline project memory

Start with [the team overview](MASTER.md) and follow its links to the guides relevant to the question. For sign-in decisions, consult [the accounts guide](AUTH.md).

Cite the relevant note and heading when answering a project question. Distinguish agreed product decisions, behaviour observed at a documented revision, planned work, and open questions. If evidence is missing or two notes disagree, flag the gap.

When the team confirms a change, follow the update guidance in the overview. Keep proposed decisions labelled until the team agrees.
